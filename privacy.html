Privacy Policy

We collect the minimum information needed to process orders, respond to inquiries and secure sign-in.

Information we collect

Your name, contact details, delivery address, order details, event enquiries and messages you voluntarily send. Email and password sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.

How information is used

To respond to inquiries, confirm availability, fulfill delivery requests, contact you about orders and prevent automated abuse. Secure session cookies keep you signed in.

Service providers

We use Firebase for authentication and database storage, Firebase to send account recovery emails, and our website hosting provider to serve the app. We do not sell customer conversations or order information to advertisers.

Retention and access

Expired OTPs cannot be used. Order and inquiry records may be retained for operational, support and legally required records. Contact the store if you want to request correction or deletion where permitted by law.

Security

Access to store operations is restricted. Avoid sharing OTPs or passwords; Sokal Bela staff will not ask you to reveal a verification code.
