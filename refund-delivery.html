Refund & Delivery Policy

We want your vegetables to arrive in the condition and quantity agreed when the order was confirmed.

Delivery area and charges

Our local base is Rampurhat. Minimum order: ₹200. Delivery is free on orders of ₹300+ within 5 km of the store. For other locations, ask us to confirm availability and applicable charges before dispatch.

24/7 orders

We're available to receive urgent orders any time. Actual delivery timing depends on stock, location, weather and rider availability; a request is not a guaranteed delivery slot.

Missing or unsatisfactory produce

Tell us as soon as possible, ideally when receiving your delivery, and share the order number and a photo where relevant. We will review your concern and arrange a replacement, adjustment or refund where appropriate.

Refund method

Approved refunds will be coordinated using the original payment method where feasible or a mutually agreed alternative. Timing depends on payment mode and the receiving institution.

Changes to event bookings

Bulk and catering cancellations are handled under the specific written quotation agreed for that event. Please contact us early if plans change.
