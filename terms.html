Terms of Service

These terms explain how Sokal Belar Bazar - 24/7 handles orders for local produce and event services.

Orders and prices

Product prices and availability may change with the daily market. Prices displayed at checkout are provisional until our team confirms the order. Your order is subject to availability and serviceable delivery location.

Minimum order and delivery

Minimum order ₹200. Free delivery is offered on eligible orders of ₹300 or more within 5 km of the store. Beyond that radius, our team will discuss availability and any delivery fee before confirming.

Payment

We support cash on delivery or a mutually confirmed payment method. Never transfer an advance based on an unverified message or third-party link. Event advances, if any, are agreed individually in writing.

Bulk and catering

Submitting an inquiry does not reserve stock, a date, a caterer or a price. We will confirm quantities, venue, timing and quotation directly before the booking is final.

Changes and cancellations

Please contact the store as early as possible about changes. Orders already packed or dispatched may not be cancellable; event terms are confirmed with the individual quotation.
