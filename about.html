About Sokal Bela / সকাল বেলা

We believe fresh, fairly priced local produce should be easy for every Rampurhat household to buy.

Rooted in Birbhum

We connect regional Birbhum farmers directly with the families and kitchens they serve. Seasonal availability and local market rates guide the day's selection.

Woman-owned and inclusive

Sokal Bela is woman-owned and welcomes the LGBTQ+ community. Our market is for everyone, with respectful service as a basic promise.

Homes and occasions

Choose everyday vegetables, ask about urgent 24/7 service, or request bulk vegetable and catering quotes for weddings, birthdays and office events.

Our local promise

Our team confirms each order's availability, current price and delivery timing before dispatch. We serve Rampurhat and surrounding parts of Birbhum.
