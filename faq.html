Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order?

₹200 for regular deliveries. Orders of ₹300+ qualify for free delivery within 5 km.

Are you open throughout the day and night?

Yes, Sokal Belar Bazar - 24/7 is available to receive enquiries and emergency requests 24/7. Delivery slot and stock availability are confirmed with the team.

How do I sign in?

Choose Login on the storefront, enter your email and receive a six-digit code. It is valid for five minutes and may be used only once. You can request another after 30 seconds.

Where do your vegetables come from?

We source regional produce and work to connect Birbhum farmers with Rampurhat households.

Can I order vegetables for a wedding?

Yes, we handle bulk vegetable and catering inquiries for weddings, birthdays, anniversaries and corporate gatherings. Tell us the date, venue and guest count to request a quote.

How can I report a delivery issue?

Contact us promptly with your order number. See our refund and delivery policy for next steps.
